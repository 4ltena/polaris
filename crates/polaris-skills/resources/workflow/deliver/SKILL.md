---
name: deliver
description: Report completed work, verification, limits, and the next task without overstating completion.
---

Report the changes actually completed, their verification, their limits, and the next task concisely. Update existing handoff material when it is within the assigned scope. Follow the authorization boundary for publishing, pushing, merging, and related actions. Do not report unfinished work as complete or create unnecessary follow-up work.

For maintenance within scope, periodically inventory obsolete build outputs and disposable generated files. Protect running or referenced artifacts, current releases, and irreplaceable evidence. Delete only confirmed targets under existing permissions, then verify removal and free space. Age alone does not establish that an artifact is disposable.
