---
name: review
description: Review an assigned specification or diff read-only using evidence from real execution paths.
---

Past a file or two, delegate the reading to reviewer and judge what it returns; below that, read it yourself. Support findings with real call paths and cover regressions, data loss, authorization drift, and missing tests. Do not target a finding count. For each finding, give its location, impact, and a concrete repair direction. Do not change code or approve your own work.
