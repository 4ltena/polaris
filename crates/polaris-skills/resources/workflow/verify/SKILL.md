---
name: verify
description: Check acceptance criteria and report commands, results, coverage, and constraints accurately.
---

Run required checks and focused verification that map to the acceptance criteria; record commands, results, and coverage. Keep failures, unrun checks, and environment constraints distinct from passing results. Once checks pass, broaden or repeat them only for new changes, failures, or unresolved concerns affecting acceptance. Compare measurements under the same conditions and account for total usage. Do not claim quality or performance that the evidence cannot explain.
